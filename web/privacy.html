Privacy

QuickViz is a personal project by Francisco Ríos. This page says what it keeps about you and why. Last updated 26 October 2026.

What is kept

  • If you sign in with Google, your email address, your name and your Google account id. They identify your account and your daily credits.
  • A sign-in cookie. It keeps you signed in for up to 30 days. Signing out removes it.
  • A record of each AI call. It holds the time, its cost, and your account or your IP address. This counts your daily credits and stops misuse.

What is not kept

  • The files, pictures and text you give QuickViz are not stored on the server. They are read, sent to the AI models and then dropped.
  • Your charts are saved only in your own browser, so a reload does not lose them. They are cleared after 7 days or when you start over.
  • There are no ads, no tracking and no analytics cookies.

Who else sees your data

  • Your data and messages go to AI models through OpenRouter, which passes them to Anthropic, Google or Typesafe. Their own privacy terms apply.
  • The site runs on Render. Accounts and credit records are stored on Neon.
  • Google handles sign-in. QuickViz only asks Google for your email and basic profile.

Never upload sensitive or personal data.

Deleting your data

Write to franrios9930@gmail.com and your account and its records will be deleted.
